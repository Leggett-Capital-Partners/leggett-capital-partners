Summer Internship Program

Each summer, Leggett and its portfolio companies bring a small cohort of interns into our Houston office. Over eight weeks you will work on real projects alongside our investment, energy, real estate and operations teams, and see firsthand how an opportunistic private equity firm sources, evaluates and manages opportunities across oil & gas, real estate and other alternatives.

Apply now See the roles

At a glance

The program

DatesJune 1 to July 31, 2026
Schedule9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, onsite
LocationHouston, Texas
Compensation$20 per hour
Apply byFebruary 20, 2026

This is a paid, onsite, eight-week program with flexibility for time off. Interns commit roughly 20 hours per week, with the opportunity for additional hours, and are matched to one of three tracks based on their background and interests. Every intern owns a major project that runs the length of the summer, supported by shorter assignments, mentorship and regular feedback.

The roles

Internship tracks

Investments

Leggett Ventures

Support venture operations and investment work across real estate, energy and alternative assets, with insight into how we evaluate opportunities and run day to day workflows.

  • Investment research across real estate, energy and alternatives
  • Market, competitive and underwriting research
  • Organizing and evaluating deal materials
  • Tracking portfolio KPIs, reporting and follow-ups
  • Operational projects that improve systems and workflows

Study: Finance, Economics, Business or a related field

Oil & Gas

Cannon Field

Join the energy team for hands-on work in mineral rights and account management. Cannon Field buys and sells mineral and non-operated interests across the lower 48, spanning more than 105,000 acres and 7,000 wells in 243 counties across 20 states.

  • Research and analyze mineral rights projects
  • Support account management and client communication
  • Prepare reports and presentations on project progress
  • Conduct market and competitive research in oil & gas
  • Track milestones and maintain project records

Study: Energy, Business, Geology, Engineering or a related field

Operations + Marketing

On Point Custom Homes

Join the real estate and marketing team for hands-on experience in luxury custom home building, development and residential marketing strategy.

  • Create and manage content for social, email and the website
  • Market research, competitor benchmarking and positioning
  • Coordinate site visits, vendor meetings and client appointments
  • Build client-facing collateral and document construction progress
  • Maintain project management systems and documentation

Study: Marketing, Real Estate, Business or a related field. A valid driver's license and reliable transportation are required for this track.

Who we look for

Qualifications

  • Rising junior or senior pursuing a bachelor's degree in a relevant field
  • Strong written and verbal communication
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, with the ability to pick up new tools quickly
  • Self-driven, collaborative, highly organized and detail-oriented
  • A genuine interest in finance, energy, real estate or private equity
  • Able to work onsite in Houston for the full internship period

What to expect

The eight weeks

Week 1

Orientation, onboarding and team introductions. Your major project is assigned.

Weeks 2 to 7

A summer-long major project, plus short minor projects and team building.

Week 4

Mid-point evaluations and feedback.

Week 6

Professional development workshop: resume, networking and interview skills.

Week 8

Final intern presentations to the team and leadership.

Why intern with us

What you take away

Hands-on experience across private equity, energy and real estate. Mentorship and networking with industry professionals. Real-world project management, and professional development you can carry into your career.
