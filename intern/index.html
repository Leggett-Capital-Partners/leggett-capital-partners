Summer Internship Program

Each summer, Leggett Capital Partners and its portfolio companies hire a small class of interns in our Houston office. You will join our private equity team and operating companies, own a real project from the first week, and apply AI to live investment and operating work across real estate, oil & gas and strategic investments.

At a glance

The program

DatesJune 1 to July 30, 2027
ScheduleMon to Fri, 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM, onsite
LocationHouston, Texas
Compensation$22 per hour
ApplicationsRolling, apply early

This is a paid, onsite summer program of roughly eight weeks, Monday through Friday (about 30 hours per week), with flexibility for scheduled time off. We hire a small class and match each intern to one of three tracks based on their background and interests. Every intern owns a real project from week one, presents it at the end of the summer, and uses AI tools daily throughout.

This is an AI-forward internship. We look for students who already use AI every day and want to apply it to live investment and operating work, building and testing tools the whole team can use.

The roles

Internship tracks

Investments

Leggett Capital Partners

Join our private equity team and work across real estate, energy and strategic investments, owning a project from the first week.

  • Research and evaluate new real estate, energy and strategic opportunities
  • Build market research, comparables and underwriting inputs for investment memos
  • Organize deal materials and prepare clear summaries for review
  • Track portfolio KPIs, reporting deadlines and investor reporting
  • Use AI daily and build tools that speed up the team's work

Study: Finance, Economics, Accounting, Business or a related field. Communications majors with a business minor are welcome.

Oil & Gas / Minerals

Cannon Field

Join the Cannon Field minerals team on live acquisitions. Cannon Field buys and sells mineral rights and non-operated interests across the lower 48, holding over 105,000 acres and 7,000 wells in 243 counties across 20 states.

  • Support mineral and royalty acquisitions: sourcing, research and valuation
  • Work buyer and seller outreach and move deals through the pipeline
  • Research ownership, acreage and well data, and build maps and summaries
  • Analyze market activity and pricing across basins
  • Use AI daily and build tools that speed up the team's work

Study: Energy Management, Petroleum Engineering, Geology, Land Management, Finance, Business or a related field. Comfortable talking with buyers and sellers by phone.

Operations + Marketing

On Point Custom Homes

Join the operations and marketing team for hands-on work in luxury custom home building and residential real estate, in the office and in the field.

  • Create and manage content for social, email and the website
  • Document construction progress with photos, video and field updates
  • Market research, competitor benchmarking and product positioning
  • Coordinate site visits, vendor meetings and client appointments
  • Use AI to speed up content, research and documentation

Study: Marketing, Real Estate, Business or a related field. A valid driver's license and reliable transportation are required for this track.

Who we look for

Qualifications

  • Rising junior or senior pursuing a bachelor's degree in a relevant field
  • A daily, hands-on AI user who has built workflows, automations or small tools and can walk us through them
  • A self-starter who takes initiative in a fast-moving, multi-business environment
  • Strong written and verbal communication
  • Proficiency in Excel and Microsoft Office, and quick to learn new tools
  • Strong organization and attention to detail
  • Interest in private equity, real estate, energy or investing
  • Able to work onsite in Houston for the full program (housing and relocation are not provided)

What to expect

What the summer looks like

Your project

A real project from week one, owned with a manager, that you present at the end of the summer.

Team case study

The intern class tackles one firm problem together and presents it jointly to leadership.

First week

A structured start on how the firm works: our investments, operating companies and the people behind them.

Lunch and learns

Weekly sessions with firm leaders, and each intern leads one of their own.

Along the way

Weekly check-ins, site visits including On Point Custom Homes builds, and team events.

Why intern with us

What you take away

Real work on live deals and portfolio companies, not busywork. Direct exposure to firm leadership and our investment process. Hands-on experience applying AI inside a working investment firm, and networking across private equity, real estate, energy and homebuilding. Strong performers may be considered for full-time opportunities after graduation.
