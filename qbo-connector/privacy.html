Privacy Policy

Leggett QBO Connector. Effective October 7, 2026.

This policy explains how Bakers Creek Management LLC, doing business as Leggett Capital Partners ("Leggett," "we," "us"), handles information accessed through the Leggett QBO Connector (the "App"). The App is an internal tool used by Leggett's accounting team. It is not offered to the public.

Which companies the App connects to

The App connects only to QuickBooks Online companies owned or administered by Leggett and its affiliated entities, and only after an administrator of that company authorizes it through Intuit.

Information we access

  • Accounting data in the connected company: chart of accounts, classes, locations, transactions, journal entries, and financial reports.
  • Names of customers, vendors, and other parties as they appear in that accounting data.
  • Basic company information, such as the company name and accounting preferences (for example, the books closing date).
  • Authorization tokens issued by Intuit for the connection.

We do not access payroll, payments, or banking login credentials through the App.

How we use it

We use this information only for Leggett's internal accounting and reporting: preparing financial statements, reconciliations, tax and audit work, and preparing journal entries for review. Journal entries are written to QuickBooks only after a member of the accounting team reviews and explicitly approves each one.

How we store and protect it

  • Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on systems controlled by Leggett. Application keys are kept in Leggett's managed password vault.
  • Report output is stored on Leggett-controlled systems and in Leggett's own Microsoft 365 environment.
  • Every request the App makes is recorded in an internal activity log.
  • Access is limited to authorized Leggett personnel.

Sharing

We do not sell this information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that process it on our behalf under contract, including AI assistant services Leggett has approved for business use (such as Anthropic's Claude), which help the accounting team analyze and summarize reports;
  • with our auditors, tax preparers, and advisers, as part of their engagement;
  • when required by law, regulation, or legal process.

Retention

We keep accounting information as long as needed for accounting, tax, audit, and regulatory record-keeping obligations. When a company is disconnected, its authorization tokens stop working and are deleted from our systems.

Disconnecting

An administrator of a connected QuickBooks Online company can disconnect the App at any time from the app management area in QuickBooks Online, or by contacting us. Disconnecting stops all further access.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
