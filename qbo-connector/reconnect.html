Reconnect QuickBooks

The connection between this QuickBooks Online company and the Leggett QBO Connector needs to be renewed.

Reconnecting is done by the Leggett accounting team together with an administrator of the company. It takes about a minute.

What to do

  1. Email jackson@leggettcp.com with the name of the company.
  2. The accounting team will send a sign-in link. An administrator of the company signs in to Intuit, selects the company, and clicks Connect.

Connections need renewing when they have not been used for 100 days, when an administrator disconnected the App, or when Intuit's five-year authorization limit is reached.
