QuickBooks in Claude

Ask Claude for reports and checks across all 77 of our QuickBooks companies, without logging in to QuickBooks. Setup takes about two minutes.

Before you start

You need a seat on the Leggett Claude Team plan and a role on the connector. Jackson assigns roles. If you are not sure you have one, ask him before connecting.

RoleWhat it allows
ReaderAll reports and checks
DrafterReader, plus drafting journal entries for review (drafts only)
AdminDrafter, plus connecting new QuickBooks companies

Connect, one time

  1. Open your connectorsIn Claude (web or desktop), go to Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Find Leggett QBO and click ConnectIt is already listed for everyone in the Leggett workspace. You do not need a link or a QuickBooks login.
  3. Sign in with MicrosoftUse your @leggettcp.com account. You land back in Claude when it is done.
  4. Check it worksStart a new chat and ask: List my QuickBooks entities.

Things to ask

  • Balance sheet for Gamble Ridge LLC as of 9/30/2026
  • P&L for the Hillman companies for Q3 2026, side by side
  • Which companies still have open bank recs for September?
  • Run the intercompany tie-out as of 9/30/2026 and list what does not tie
  • Scan all companies for uncategorized and suspense balances
  • Compare Q3 to Q2 for Cannon Field LLC and flag big changes

Large results come back as a CSV download link. Links expire after 30 minutes, so download them when you need them.

Ground rules

  • The connector reads QuickBooks. It never changes it on its own.
  • Journal entries are drafts with a preview. Nothing is posted without a person approving that preview, and posting to our live companies is switched off.
  • Every request is logged with your name.
  • Keep results inside Leggett. Do not paste financial data into tools or chats outside our Microsoft 365 and Claude workspace.

If something goes wrong

"You do not have the role" or access denied
Your role is missing or was just added. Ask Jackson to check, then disconnect and reconnect.
"Authorization lapsed" for a company
That one company needs a QuickBooks admin to reconnect it. Tell Jackson which company.
Leggett QBO is not in your connectors list
Your Claude account may not be in the Leggett workspace. Ask Jackson.
A company is missing
An Admin can add it in a few minutes. Send Jackson the company name.
