Leggett QBO Connector

An internal tool that connects the Leggett Capital Partners accounting team to the QuickBooks Online companies it manages.

Internal use only. This app is not offered to the public. It connects only to QuickBooks Online companies owned or administered by Leggett Capital Partners and its affiliated entities, and only when an administrator of that company authorizes it.

What it does

  • Reads accounting reports (trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, general ledger, agings) so the team can prepare financial statements across entities without downloading each one by hand.
  • Checks reports against each other, for example tying the balance sheet to the trial balance.
  • Prepares journal entries for review. Nothing is posted to QuickBooks unless a member of the accounting team reviews the entry and explicitly approves it.

How a company is connected

A QuickBooks administrator of the company signs in through Intuit and approves access. The connection can be removed at any time from within QuickBooks Online, or by contacting us.
